Frank statecharts
Mouse
  • Drag a state to move it; drop it into a group to put it inside.
  • Drag the small circle on a state's right edge onto another state to add a transition.
  • Double-click empty space (or inside a group) for a new state.
  • Drag the background to pan; scroll to zoom.
  • Drag the blue corner of a selected state to resize it.
Keys
  • Delete the selection, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y undo/redo, Ctrl+S save, F fit, Esc deselect.
Lines
  • Solid: an event. Dashed: a timer (after). Dotted: a condition (when). A loop with a dot: actions only, no change of state.
  • A black dot and arrow mark a group's first state.
Simulate runs the chart on a clock of its own (green = active). Vibe asks Claude to change it; you see what changed before you accept. Saved charts are picked up by the running face engine within a second.
